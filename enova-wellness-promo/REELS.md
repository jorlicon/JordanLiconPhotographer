# Social reels — 4 x vertical, 10-20s each

Not yet built as HyperFrames compositions — planned here first since they
depend on the same missing footage as the master film (`MISSING-ASSETS.md`).
Build these as separate `compositions/reels/<n>-<slug>.html` + a
`reel-<n>.html` host once real footage is available; each is its own short
edit, not a cut-down of the master's six frames.

All four: 1080x1920, burned-in captions, no clean version required (captions
are the point on these). Same color grade and typography as the master
(BRIEF.md § Customizations).

## Reel 1 — The Enova Experience (10-20s)

Arc: arrival → welcome → consultation → treatment → final glow → booking CTA.
This is the "whole journey in miniature" reel — pulls one beat from each
master frame. On-screen text: opens with "Enova Wellness Center," closes with
"Schedule your personalized experience."

## Reel 2 — Advanced Aesthetics (10-20s)

Arc: consultation → facial mapping → injectables (tasteful/discreet) →
Morpheus8 → PRP/PDRN → provider expertise → philosophy line. On-screen text
closes on "Enhance, never overdo." Needs `MISSING-ASSETS.md` items 14-16, 20.

## Reel 3 — Facial and Skincare Treatments (10-20s)

Arc: products → cleansing → serum application → treatment device → relaxation
→ Camryn's final beauty reveal. Lightest/most approachable of the four —
good top-of-funnel reel. Needs items 11-13, 22-25.

## Reel 4 — Massage and Body Wellness (10-20s)

Arc: massage oil → therapeutic touch → body treatment → peaceful detail shots
→ El Paso Country Club environment. Slowest pacing of the four, leans into
the location's exclusivity. Needs items 4, 17-18, 1-3.

## Build order recommendation

Once footage lands, build Reel 1 first — it's the general-purpose reel and
will surface any shared setup issues (captions, vertical crop, grade) before
committing to the other three.
