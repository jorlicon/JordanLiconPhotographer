## Intent

A premium, cinematic brand video for Enova Wellness Center, a private beauty,
wellness, and advanced-aesthetics destination inside the El Paso Country Club.
Featured model: Camryn Heon. Production company: Jordan Licon Photography.

Tone: elegant, peaceful, trustworthy, exclusive, modern, medically responsible.
Luxury spa tranquility combined with advanced aesthetic treatments and
personalized professional care. Brand philosophy: "Enhance, never overdo."

Full six-beat structure (0:00-0:06 location/intro, 0:06-0:16 personalized
experience, 0:16-0:30 treatments/technology montage, 0:30-0:43 trust/natural
results, 0:43-0:53 emotional transformation, 0:53-0:60 closing CTA), voiceover
lines, and on-screen text per beat are locked from the client brief verbatim —
see `STORYBOARD.md` for the frame-by-frame breakdown.

## Assets

**None supplied to this project yet.** The source project ("ENOVA MED SPA -
CAMRYN") is open in a local DaVinci Resolve project on the user's own machine
and is not reachable from here. Every shot in `STORYBOARD.md` and
`compositions/` is built as a clearly labeled placeholder until real footage,
photography, and brand assets are exported and added. See `MISSING-ASSETS.md`
for the exact list of what's needed and where each item goes.

## Customizations

- Deliverables: 16:9 master, 9:16 adaptation, burned-in captions on the
  vertical version, a clean (caption-free) version, one thumbnail/cover-frame
  recommendation, and four additional vertical reels (10-20s each): "The Enova
  Experience," "Advanced Aesthetics," "Facial and Skincare Treatments,"
  "Massage and Body Wellness."
- Color/brand direction: warm, refined grade — natural skin tones, soft
  highlights, clean whites, muted greens, cream, subtle gold accents.
- Typography: clean, minimal, elegant. One clear idea per title. Motion
  graphics inspired by Enova's green/cream/gold palette.
- Audio: elegant, calming, modern wellness music with a subtle cinematic
  build — premium and contemporary, not corporate or overly spiritual.
  Properly licensed only. Subtle natural sound where available (doors,
  product application, towels, water, massage oils, devices, room ambience).
- Editing: elegant cinematic pacing, smooth cuts, restrained speed ramps,
  gentle dissolves, motivated transitions on movement. Calm open, energy
  build through the treatment montage, peaceful emotional finish. Tactile
  close-ups (hands, products, textures, devices) balanced against wider
  establishing shots.

## Notes

**Hard constraints from the client brief — treat as non-negotiable:**

- Use only supplied Enova footage, photographs, logos, and approved brand
  assets. Never invent or substitute stock/generic footage as if it were
  Enova's own.
- Do not invent medical procedures, results, testimonials, credentials,
  prices, or treatment claims.
- Do not present staged footage as a real medical procedure.
- No before/after comparisons unless matching approved images are supplied.
- No guaranteed-result language, anywhere in copy or on-screen text.
- Injectable/medical imagery must stay tasteful, discreet, professionally
  presented — avoid graphic medical imagery entirely.
- Remove any visible personal or medical information from any real footage
  before it's used.
- **Missing shot or asset → insert a clearly labeled placeholder and list it
  in `MISSING-ASSETS.md`. Never generate misleading footage to fill a gap.**
- Avoid a generic spa-commercial feel — this must read as local, exclusive,
  human, and specifically made for Enova.
