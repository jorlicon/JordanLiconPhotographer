## Intent

A cinematic 20-second showcase reel of Jordan Licon Photography's El Paso
studio itself — the physical space, not a sales pitch. A walkthrough of the
industrial-chic loft: big factory windows and warm window light, the natural-
light room with vintage props (Coca-Cola machine, pampas grass), the cyc-wall
lighting studio with grip gear, the raw exposed-beam warehouse space, and the
lounge/kitchen area. Tone: warm, moody, professional — the kind of space that
makes a photographer want to shoot there. Show it as-is; let the room speak.
