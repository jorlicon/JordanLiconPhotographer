# Asset inventory

No live site capture (network capture failed); assets are the 5 studio
photos supplied directly in the brief, plus the site's own logo pulled from
this repo's `assets/brand/`. All images are portrait source stills
(1932x2576) except `04-warehouse.jpg` (landscape, 2576x1932) — needs a
cover-fit crop for the 9:16 frame.

## capture/assets/01-window-room.jpg
Natural-light room: large multi-pane factory window with a wood sill,
vintage red Coca-Cola vending machine at left, dried pampas grass in vases
on the sill, a single wrought-iron stool centered on worn wood floor,
exposed brick wall, wood-plank ceiling with visible pipes. Warm afternoon
light pooling on the floor. Strong establishing-shot candidate — quiet,
graphic, symmetrical window composition.

## capture/assets/02-loft-wide.jpg
Wide view of the same natural-light loft: exposed metal ductwork and red
fire-suppression pipe along the ceiling, hanging bulb fixtures on branches,
tall factory windows with sheer white curtains, a vintage iron daybed and
leather chaise staged near the windows, pampas grass, dark wood floor
running the length of the room. Reads as the "hero" wide establishing shot
of the studio's main shooting floor.

## capture/assets/03-lighting-corner.jpg
The working lighting studio: a tan/beige seamless paper backdrop on the
left, a white cyclorama curve on the right with a softbox and grip stand
in frame, additional strobes/umbrellas and a rolling grip cart in the
foreground, warm pendant lights overhead. This is the "gear/production"
beat — shows the studio is shoot-ready, not just pretty.

## capture/assets/04-warehouse.jpg
Raw, unfinished second warehouse space: rows of wood columns and beams
receding into darkness, painted brick walls, a moody green/cool cast from
mixed window and shop light. Landscape orientation. Good as a brief
atmospheric/scale beat — conveys the building's scope beyond the finished
studio rooms.

## capture/assets/05-lounge-kitchen.jpg
Client lounge/kitchen area: brown leather cowhide sectional sofa in the
foreground with warm sunset light hitting the cushions, stainless
refrigerator and dark wood dresser against the far wall, the same ceiling
pipe/bulb fixtures, and a glimpse of the shooting floor with lighting gear
beyond. Good closing/"between takes" beat before the brand card.

## capture/assets/jl-logo-512.png
Jordan Licon Photography's own logo mark (from `assets/brand/` in this
repo). Use on the closing brand card.
