Headshot questions

What does a Jordan Licon headshot session include?

A Jordan Licon headshot session includes consultation, professional lighting, expression coaching, proofing, retouching, and usage-ready final files. Individual sessions start at $350 and include one finalized headshot with up to three outfits. Additional finalized headshots are available for $200 each, making the session useful for LinkedIn, company websites, actor submissions, pageantry, speaker bios, press kits, and personal branding.

Who books headshots with Jordan Licon Photography?

Jordan Licon Photography works with executives, entrepreneurs, actors, pageant titleholders, corporate teams, medical practices, attorneys, real estate professionals, public speakers, and professionals who need a polished online presence. Sessions are directed around confidence, expression, wardrobe, crop, and final use so the portrait feels credible and intentional rather than casual or generic.
