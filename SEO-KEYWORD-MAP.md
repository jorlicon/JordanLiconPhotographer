## Decision rules

1. A service page owns purchase-ready local queries. An article answers a narrower informational question and links to the service page.
2. Do not create a new location-service page unless Search Console shows a distinct query group and the business can provide specific work, process, proof, and a fitting call to action.
3. Consolidate two articles only after Search Console shows sustained query overlap or one page consistently receives no impressions while the other earns the same topic.
4. Keep portfolio pages focused on proof and service pages focused on selection, scope, process, and inquiry.
5. Measure non-branded impressions, qualified organic visits, and confirmed inquiries by landing page. Rankings alone do not identify whether the traffic is useful.
