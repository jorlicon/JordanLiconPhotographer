<article>
  <h1>Professional Headshot Photography Guide: What Makes a Great Headshot</h1>
  <div class="meta">Published May 5, 2026 | By Jordan Licon Photography</div>

  <p>A professional headshot is one of the most important images in your professional brand. Whether you're building a LinkedIn presence, launching a business, or pursuing an acting career, your headshot communicates credibility, approachability, and professionalism before you say a word.</p>

  <h2>What is a Professional Headshot?</h2>
  <p>A professional headshot is a portrait photograph specifically designed for business, career, or professional purposes. Unlike casual photos, professional headshots are strategically composed, professionally lit, and carefully edited to present you in the best possible light while maintaining authenticity.</p>

  <p>Professional headshots are used for:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>LinkedIn profiles</strong> - Create a strong first impression in your professional network</li>
    <li><strong>Corporate websites</strong> - Appear credible and approachable on your company's leadership or team pages</li>
    <li><strong>Marketing materials</strong> - Use in brochures, case studies, and promotional content</li>
    <li><strong>Casting and auditions</strong> - Represent your range and personality to casting directors</li>
    <li><strong>Press and media</strong> - Provide journalists with a professional image of you</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>The 5 Elements of a Great Professional Headshot</h2>

  <h3>1. Professional Lighting</h3>
  <p>Lighting is the foundation of every great headshot. Professional lighting flatters your facial features, creates dimension in your portrait, and highlights your eyes. Studio lighting is carefully controlled to eliminate unflattering shadows.</p>

  <h3>2. Clear Expression and Direction</h3>
  <p>Your expression is what people will connect with most. Professional photographers guide you toward natural, confident expressions that convey professionalism and approachability.</p>

  <h3>3. Clean, Simple Composition</h3>
  <p>The strongest professional headshots use simple, uncluttered composition. The background is neutral, the framing focuses on your face and shoulders, and all attention is on you.</p>

  <h3>4. Professional Retouching</h3>
  <p>Professional retouching enhances your headshot while keeping you looking like yourself. Good retouching evens out skin tone, minimizes blemishes, and maintains natural appearance.</p>

  <h3>5. Proper Image Dimensions and Format</h3>
  <p>A professional headshot is delivered in formats suitable for different uses: square for LinkedIn, 8x10 for printing, and high-resolution for print quality.</p>

  <h2>How to Choose a Professional Headshot Photographer</h2>

  <h3>Review the Portfolio</h3>
  <p>Look for consistency in lighting quality, variety in subject types, and professional retouching. Choose a photographer who understands your industry and what your clients or casting directors expect to see.</p>

  <h3>Look for Direction and Coaching Ability</h3>
  <p>The best headshot photographers are excellent at directing clients. They'll guide you toward natural, confident expressions and help you feel comfortable in front of the camera.</p>

  <h3>Verify Professional Facilities</h3>
  <p>Professional headshots require professional facilities with quality lighting equipment and professional retouching workflow.</p>

  <h2>Service-Specific Headshot Photography</h2>

  <p>Different professional paths require different headshot approaches:</p>

  <p><strong><a href="/executive-headshots-el-paso">Executive Headshots</a></strong> - For business leaders and executives who need headshots that convey authority and professionalism.</p>

  <p><strong><a href="/actor-headshots-el-paso">Actor Headshots</a></strong> - For performers and actors who need casting-friendly headshots showing range and personality.</p>

  <p><strong><a href="/corporate-team-headshots-el-paso">Corporate Team Headshots</a></strong> - For organizations that need consistent, professional headshots across all team members.</p>

  <h2>Final Thoughts</h2>
  <p>A professional headshot is an investment in your career. By understanding what makes a great headshot and choosing an experienced photographer, you'll have images that work as hard as you do.</p>

  <div class="meta">Published May 5, 2026 | By Jordan Licon Photography</div>

  <p>Your LinkedIn profile photo is one of the most important elements of your professional brand on the platform. It's the first thing people see when they view your profile, and it significantly impacts whether they choose to connect with you.</p>

  <div class="stat-box">

  <h2>Why Your LinkedIn Profile Photo Matters</h2>
  <p>LinkedIn is a professional networking platform where first impressions matter. Your LinkedIn profile photo is your professional brand in image form. It impacts whether you're discovered, trusted, and contacted for opportunities.</p>

  <h2>The Ideal LinkedIn Profile Photo Specifications</h2>

  <h3>Technical Requirements</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>File format:</strong> JPG, GIF, or PNG</li>
    <li><strong>Minimum size:</strong> 400 x 400 pixels</li>
    <li><strong>Aspect ratio:</strong> Square (1:1) is best for full visibility</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Composition Recommendations</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Head and shoulders only:</strong> Crop to show head, neck, and shoulders</li>
    <li><strong>Face prominence:</strong> Your face should be clearly visible and fill most of the frame</li>
    <li><strong>Direct eye contact:</strong> Look straight at the camera with a confident, professional expression</li>
    <li><strong>Natural expression:</strong> Smile naturally—avoid looking too serious or overly casual</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>What Makes a Great LinkedIn Profile Photo</h2>

  <h3>Professional Lighting</h3>
