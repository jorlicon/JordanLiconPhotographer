Privacy Policy

Jordan Licon Photography collects only the information needed to respond to inquiries, schedule sessions, prepare proposals, deliver services, and communicate with clients. This policy applies to information submitted through the website, email, booking links, inquiry forms, and project communication related to photography, cinematography, headshots, architecture, drone, food, portrait, and commercial production services.

Information collected

Inquiry forms and emails may include your name, email address, phone number, company, project details, timeline, budget range, location, intended usage, preferred service, and any notes you choose to share. If you book a session through a third-party scheduling or studio-management platform, that platform may also collect information needed to process the booking, agreement, invoice, or payment.

How information is used

Information is used to answer questions, prepare estimates, schedule photography or cinematography services, deliver project materials, maintain client communication, and keep accurate project records. Project details may also be used internally to plan production logistics such as locations, crew needs, licensing, timelines, styling, retouching, delivery requirements, and image usage.

Sharing

Client information is not sold. Information may be shared only with trusted production partners when needed for a project, such as assistants, stylists, retouchers, makeup artists, booking platforms, payment processors, or delivery platforms. Those partners receive only the information reasonably necessary to complete their role in the project.

Website analytics and third-party tools

The website may use hosting, analytics, contact-form, booking, and media tools that collect standard technical information such as browser type, referring pages, general location, device information, and interaction data. This information helps improve site performance, security, navigation, and the clarity of service pages. Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. These tools are used to support basic business operations, not to sell visitor information.

Retention and requests

Client communications and project records may be retained for business, tax, licensing, portfolio, and client-service purposes. You may request an update or correction to your contact information by emailing the studio. Certain records may need to be retained when required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@jordanliconphotography.com.

Photography-specific privacy context

Photography projects may involve visual references, location notes, production schedules, names of team members, brand guidelines, wardrobe information, delivery preferences, and licensing details. Those details are used only to plan and deliver the requested work. Portfolio publication, client credit, testimonials, and behind-the-scenes use are handled according to the project agreement and client communication.
